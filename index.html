FAQ & Contact

What vaccinations are required?

Dogs and cats must have an up-to-date rabies vaccine. Bring the vaccine card on the day.

How long does a groom take?

A Full Groom usually takes 2–4 hours depending on size and coat. Bath & Blow Dry takes 1–2 hours.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes, from My Appointments. A cancellation request must include a reason and is not final until the salon reviews it. If the salon declines it, you will see their explanation in My Appointments. Please give at least 24 hours' notice.

My pet's coat is badly matted.

Add De-matting. Severe matting may need a short shave-down for your pet's comfort; the groomer will discuss it with you.

Which size should I choose?

Small up to 7 kg, Medium 7–15 kg, Large 15–30 kg, X-Large 30 kg and above.
